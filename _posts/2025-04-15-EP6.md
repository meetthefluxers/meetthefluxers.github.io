<p> We speak with a field geologist and an external affairs specialist from a climate tech company working on a revolutionary method of carbon sequestration that transforms mine waste into carbon-rich rock. Flux-measuring land rovers, flux towers, proprietary algorithms, and field surveys are all helping to maximize the process of terrestrial carbon mineralization at Arca’s pilot study site in Western Australia. Sean and Sam dive into the details of their work, like how to work with local communities, methodology development, and the benefits of climate action.</p>
