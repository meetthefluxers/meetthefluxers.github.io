<p>Wildfires and clear-cutting in the boreal and arctic regions can release harmful gasses into the atmosphere. Experts in fluxes from taiga and tundra ecosystems share their first-hand fieldwork experiences in this episode about the atmospheric consequences of increasingly frequent and intensifying fires.</p>
