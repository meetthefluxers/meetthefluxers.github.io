<p>Reducing greenhouse gas emissions from agriculture calls for strong collaboration between scientists and farmers to develop trust and implement meaningful changes. Learn more about the innovative practices being undertaken at a rice farm in Arkansas and what that means for the climate!</p>


<div class="ep-row">
  <div class="ep-column">
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/MarkIsbell.png" alt="Mark Isbell">
      <figcaption><b><a href = "https://www.linkedin.com/in/mark-isbell-a0897560/">Mark Isbell</a></b> is a fourth-generation rice farmer and partner at Isbell Farms & Generation Farms. Mark is a member of the USA Rice Farmers Conservation Committee and a board member of <a href="https://www.farmprogress.com/conservation-and-sustainability/arkansas-grower-named-to-usda-carbon-credits-advisory-council">Field to Market</a>, a network of organizations that values a future in which farmers, business, and nature thrive. He collaborates with the USDA and UArk to advance agricultural sustainability by monitoring fluxes with chambers and towers, evaluating water use, and even studying winter fish cultivation.</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </div>

  <div class="ep-column">
    <figure>
      <img src="/images/BeatrizMorenoGarcia.png" alt="Beatriz Moreno-García">
      <figcaption><b><a href="https://runkle.uark.edu/team/beatriz/">Beatriz Moreno-García</a></b> is a Research Assistant Professor in the Landscape Flux Group at the University of Arkansas Department of Biological & Agricultural Engineering. She analyzes fluxes, environmental and meteorological data over rice fields, with a focus on measuring greenhouse gas reduction from best practices such as alternate wetting and drying, or AWD. She also helps develop decision support tools with various agricultural applications. Beatriz is also a member of the Metrics Committee for Field to Market, a group that helps to review the 8 sustainability metrics in Fieldprint Platform. These metrics measure environmental outcomes from individual farm fields and thus help farmers to make decisions about management practices. She was awarded by Field to Market as “Trusted Adviser of the Year” in 2022 for her work supporting farmers' journeys of continuous improvement.
</figcaption>
