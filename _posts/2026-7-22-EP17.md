<p>Explore the connections between rainforests, climate, agriculture, and how uncertainty travels from the Amazon canopy to decisions made in the field. The Amazon is often described as a climate engine, influencing rainfall, clouds, and growing seasons far beyond the forest itself. At the same time, rain-fed agricultural systems must operate under increasing pressure from land use change and global climate change, where timing and reliability of rainfall can matter as much as totals. Rather than providing simple answers, the way scientific knowledge moves from flux towers to climate models, and from climate information to real-world decisions, ensures uncertainty is managed rather than eliminated.</p>
